# ADR 0005 — The book is written in English only

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Context

The source notes mix English and Portuguese. The field's tools, papers, and documentation are in English, and the book is meant for an international audience.

## Decision

All text, file names, section IDs, templates, and diagrams are in English. Portuguese notes are translated, not copied.

## Alternatives considered

- **Bilingual edition:** Quarto has no first-class multilingual book support, and maintaining two editions doubles the work.

## Consequences

- One edition to maintain.
- Terms are used in their English form, as they appear in tools and papers.
